SESSION QUESTION

How do we know a brain signal actually means something?

01

What you’ll learn

By the end of this session, you can:

  • 1Know the Academy team, semester format, resources, and participation system.
  • 2Explain why the same EEG can look different in the time and frequency domains.
  • 3Separate an interesting observation from reliable evidence.

Before you trust the difference

A visible pattern is a starting point, not a conclusion.

01

Fair comparison

Were the person, channel, duration, scale, and recording setup the same?

02

Repeatability

Does the pattern return each time the condition is repeated?

03

Alternative explanations

Could artifacts, electrode contact, recording order, or fatigue explain it?

04

New data

Does the pattern appear in another recording or another participant?
