Before you trust the difference

A visible pattern is a starting point, not a conclusion.

01

Fair comparison

Were the person, channel, duration, scale, and recording setup the same?

02

Repeatability

Does the pattern return each time the condition is repeated?

03

Alternative explanations

Could artifacts, electrode contact, recording order, or fatigue explain it?

04

New data

Does the pattern appear in another recording or another participant?
